Not: @Transactional only works when called from a controller.

It can be called from another Spring bean:

Service A
   ↓
Spring Proxy of Service B
   ↓
@Transactional method


That works because the call to Service B 
goes through Service B's proxy.

The invocation needs to cross the Spring proxy 
for proxy-based transaction interception to occur.

So: its about through proxy vs bypassing proxy
