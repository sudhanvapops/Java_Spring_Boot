1. Business Basics (Start easy)
    - When did you start this shop?
    - Why did you choose this business?
    - Is this your main source of income?
    - How many people work here (including you)?


2. Investment & Setup (MSME angle)
    - How much did you invest to start the shop?
    - Did you take any loan or self-fund it?
    - What were the major setup costs? (rent, equipment, stock)



3. Daily Revenue (Core part – don’t skip)
    - On average, how many customers do you get per day?
    - What is the average spending per customer?
    - What is your approximate daily income? (range is fine)
    - Which time of day is the busiest?


👉 If they hesitate, soften it:

“Just an approximate idea is enough for my study.”


4. Expenses & Profit
    - What are your main expenses?
    - (raw materials, rent, electricity, wages)
    - Do prices of materials affect your profit?
    - Which item gives you the highest profit?


5. Supply Chain (very important for “local economy”)
    - Where do you buy your raw materials from?
    - Are your suppliers local?
    - How often do you restock?

👉 This shows money flow between businesses


6. Customers & Demand
    - Who are your main customers? (students, families, workers)
    - Which products sell the most?
    - Do seasons affect your sales? (summer vs rainy)


7. Digital & Modern Practices
    - Do you accept UPI / online payments?
    - Which payment method is most common?
    - Do you use any apps for business?


8. Challenges (important for marks)
    - What are the biggest problems you face?
    - Do you face strong competition nearby?
    - Have costs increased in recent years?


9. Growth & Future
    - Do you want to expand your business?
    - What support would help your business grow?
    - Have you heard of MSME schemes or government support?


10. Local Impact (this is your “gold” section)
    - Does your shop help other local businesses? (suppliers, workers)
    - How does this shop support your family or employees?
    - Do you think small shops like yours are important for the area?
